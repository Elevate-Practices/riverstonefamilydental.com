Insurance and payments

We're in network, so your family is covered.

Book online(620) 442-0320

We believe in making smiles affordable. We're in network with many PPO plans, so your family can use the benefits you have.

Insurance

If you don't see your plan listed with us, there's still a good chance we're in network. Call (620) 442-0320 and we'll check your coverage and help you make the most of your benefits before your visit.

Payment plans

We're happy to help you get the dentistry you want and can set up a payment plan that fits your budget, including interest-free options and lower monthly payments. We present every option before treatment, so there are no surprises.

CareCredit

CareCredit offers special financing and low monthly payments, with no up-front costs and no prepayment penalties. Apply for CareCredit online.

No insurance?

The Riverstone Membership starts at $299 a year and covers your cleanings, exams and X-rays, with 15% off other care.
