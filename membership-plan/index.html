The Dual plan is for a parent and child, or a husband and wife. The Family plan covers up to four family members (children until age 19); additional family members are $125 each. The membership is a savings plan, not insurance.

Traditional dental insurance premiums are expensive, and benefits are limited on most plans. As a Riverstone Member, all of your preventive care (cleanings, X-rays and exams) is 100% covered in our office, and you'll save on restorative care like fillings and crowns, and even cosmetic dentistry.

Common questions

Is the membership insurance?

No. It's an annual reduced-fee savings plan with our office. No claims, deductibles, yearly maximums or waiting periods.

Can existing patients join?

Yes. It's open to new and existing patients.

Who is the Dual plan for?

Two people: a parent and child, or a husband and wife.

How does the Family plan work?

It covers up to four family members (children until age 19) for $799 a year. Additional family members are $125 each.

How do I join?

Call (620) 442-0320 or ask at your next visit.
