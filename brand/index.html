Give it room

Keep clear space around the logo on every side, at least the height of the "R" in Riverstone.

Keep it legible

At least 1.5 in wide in print and 280 px on screen. Smaller than that, use the icon.

Don't alter it

No stretching, recoloring, outlines, shadows, or older logo versions.

Voice

  • Plain and warm. Talk like a neighbor on Central Avenue, not a brochure.
  • Short sentences. One idea at a time.
  • Practical first. Lead with what patients ask: cost, insurance, how soon they can get in.
  • Familiar faces. Name the team. Val has been here since 1986 and Julie since 2000.
  • Every offer shows its value. Membership prices always say what's included.
  • Honest always. Real reviews only, word for word. No before/after photos or patient faces without consent.

Photography

Real people only

Use photos of Dr. Pieper, the team and the office. No stock photos of other practices.

Large and calm

Big frames with rounded corners. No text over faces.

Each photo once

Give every page its own photo so the site never repeats itself.
