Dr. Ben Pieper, DMD

He will always treat you like family.

Book online(620) 442-0320

Dr. Benjamin Pieper was raised as the oldest of nine children in a small Wyoming town. Before finishing college, he spent two years in Peru as a missionary for his church. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from the University of Wyoming and his Doctorate of Dental Medicine from Midwestern University in Glendale, Arizona.

Before buying Riverstone Family Dental in Arkansas City, Dr. Pieper was an associate in a busy Tulsa practice and Managing Clinical Director in Enid, building skills in caring for patients of all ages and in prosthodontics.

Dr. Pieper strives to give his patients the best possible care and is committed to advancing his knowledge in dentistry. He loves to keep learning and growing as a dental professional, and he will always treat you like family.

Dr. Pieper and his wife, Jamie, love family time exploring, hiking, running, traveling and trying anything new. He also volunteers in the schools and coaches youth soccer in the local recreation league.

He looks forward to helping you reach or keep great oral health, with the quality care you've come to expect.

  • Doctorate of Dental Medicine, Midwestern University, Glendale, Arizona
  • Bachelor of Science in Biology, University of Wyoming
  • Two years in Peru as a missionary
  • Experience in prosthodontics and patients of all ages
