Riverstone Family Dental offers toothaches & emergencies at our office on Central Avenue in Arkansas City, Kansas. Call (620) 442-0320 or book online.

Call us first

If you're in pain, call (620) 442-0320 as early in the day as you can. We work emergencies into the schedule so you can get out of pain, and we'll tell you the soonest time we have.

We can help with

  • Toothaches
  • A cracked, chipped or broken tooth
  • A lost filling or crown
  • Swelling or pain when you bite
  • A knocked-out tooth (call right away)

Until you get here

  • Rinse with warm salt water
  • Use a cold compress on your cheek for swelling
  • Keep a broken piece of tooth and bring it with you
  • Avoid chewing on the sore side

When to go to the ER

If you have swelling that makes it hard to breathe or swallow, a high fever, or an injury to your head or jaw, call 911 or go to the emergency room.

Ways to pay

We're in network with many PPO plans and will help you use your benefits. We can also set up a payment plan, and CareCredit offers low monthly payments.

Common questions

Can you see me today?

Call us first thing at (620) 442-0320. We work emergencies into the day so you can get out of pain.

I'm not a patient yet. Can I still come in?

Yes. New patients with a toothache are welcome. Tell us it's an emergency when you call.

What if it happens on a Friday or weekend?

We're open Fridays by appointment and closed weekends. Call (620) 442-0320 and follow the message for the next steps.
