Good to know.

Do you take my insurance?

We're in network with many PPO plans. If your plan isn't on our list, there's still a good chance we're in network, so call (620) 442-0320 and we'll check before your visit.

Can you see me today for a toothache?

Call us first thing. We work emergencies into the day so you can get out of pain, and we'll tell you the soonest time we have.

What if I don't have dental insurance?

Our membership plan starts at $299 a year and covers two cleanings, exams, X-rays and free whitening, with 15% off other treatment. CareCredit financing is also available.

What happens at my first visit?

You'll have a full exam with X-rays and photos of your teeth. Then Dr. Pieper walks you through what he sees and your options, with no pressure.

Do you see children?

Yes. We care for children, teens and adults, so the whole family can come to one office.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. Se habla español. Call (620) 442-0320 to make your appointment.
